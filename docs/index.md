# Emberworks

![Emberworks original forge-and-ember banner](assets/brand/banner.png)

**From charcoal to a connected workshop.** Emberworks adds industrial machines, Flux power, material routing and finite local water and chemical stores to Hytale.

This guide covers **0.3.0-dev for Hytale 0.7.2**. It describes a development build with native-tested representative machine loops. Survival acquisition, human sound/visual acceptance and real multiplayer acceptance are still incomplete. No public mod release or download is available.

[![A compact native Emberworks test workshop with copper and steel machines, a low charging deck and water intake](assets/screenshots/workshop.png)](assets/screenshots/workshop.png)

*Actual Hytale 0.7.2 development workshop. This is a creative test layout, not a claim of survival completion. The banner above is original brand illustration; the screenshots throughout this guide are native gameplay.*

Start with a Cinder Dynamo, Flux Rails, a Stamp Mill and an Arc Hearth. One iron ore becomes two grit, and those two grit become two iron bars. Later machines add timber and stone processing, carbon infusion, crystal compression, patterns, sorting and held-pack charging.

The current catalog contains **106 processing recipes**. The guide lists their shipped defaults plus 64 authored Workbench recipes. Servers and existing saves can retain different balances; the machine's own recipe panel is authoritative.

For experimental save support and the additional restrictions on water and charging, read [known limits](troubleshooting.md) before choosing a world.
