# Development changes

## 0.3.0-dev · Hytale 0.7.2

The Rotary development build adds separate finite fluid/chemical stores, paused Vaporize/Condense selection and paid 1:1 conversion in both directions. Native water/vapor/water and Hydrogen/liquid/Hydrogen loops each spent 20,000 Flux per 1,000-unit round trip. Both paid modes and stored liquid have normal reload evidence. Later chemical acquisition, full survival progression and external acceptance remain incomplete. See [Rotary conversion](features/rotary.md).

The Separator development build adds finite water input, two independent chemical outputs and saved paid work. A native whole-bucket loop, including normal paid reload, produced exactly 1,000 Hydrogen and 500 Oxygen for 10,000 Flux. Chemical tanks/tubes now retain these two gases. Brine/heavy-water processing, full survival acquisition and external acceptance remain incomplete.

The chemical-network development build adds five finite tank/tube tiers and separate world-axis chemical ports for Carbon and Cobalt binder. A native routed Carbon-alloy batch retained paid work across reload and completed exactly once. Binder rejection, return, whole-carrier recovery and stocked Foundry installation have been exercised. Complete survival progression and multiplayer acceptance remain incomplete.

The fluid-network development build adds five finite tank/pipe tiers, saved world-axis fluid ports, shared bounded flow rates and ordinary full water, milk and Mosshorn milk bucket exchange. Native water/milk transport, all three carrier identities across reload, and stocked tier upgrades have been exercised. A reproduced paused-source pull defect is fixed; natural milk acquisition and ordinary Use acceptance remain incomplete.

Current development work includes factory tiers and native inventory controls with Auto recipe selection; 106 authored processing recipes; finite carbon/Cobalt/water stores; conservative timber secondary output; exact/prefix sorting and crate locks; two native Fieldcraft patterns; direct native water intake/finite discharge; and held Flux Pack charging/returns.

The current held-pack build also fixes the native CustomUI metadata display failure while preserving authoritative inventory metadata. Native charged movement, reload and supported retained-result recovery are exercised. Audio uses original connected ElevenLabs-generated assets with state-driven bindings; human listening and multiplayer acceptance remain incomplete.

No public mod release has been published. The [support status](installation.md) and [known limits](troubleshooting.md) describe the development scope.
